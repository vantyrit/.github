# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Vantyr projects are open to everyone. We want participation to be a respectful, harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all communication
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before reacting
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Credit the work of others

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, whether public or private
- Personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate intimidation
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Vantyr repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone is representing the project or the community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow them. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report a violation, email im@sourav.com.bd. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.
